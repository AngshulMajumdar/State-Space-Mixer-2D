Benchmarks

Frozen embeddings and one linear probe for every model. ssmix2d is never trained; the probe is the only fitted component.

Protocol

  • Probe: StandardScaler, then multinomial logistic regression. C is chosen from {10−3, 10−2, 10−1, 1} by 3-fold cross-validation inside each training split. The probe is identical for every model.
  • ESC-50: 2,000 five-second clips in 50 environmental classes, official 5-fold cross-validation.
  • FSDD: Free Spoken Digit Dataset, 3,000 recordings of the digits 0–9: the official split (takes 0–4 form the test set), and leave-one-speaker-out over its six speakers (train on five, test on the unseen one).
  • ssmix2d inherits every setting of the 1-D ssmix; nothing was tuned for the 2-D model.
  • Baselines are those of the State-Space-Mixer suite, run on the same prepared audio with the same probe. Pretrained models use the authors' released weights.

Mixing along frequency adds 3.8 points on speakers the probe has never heard: pitch and formant differences between speakers are shifts along the mel axis, which the 2-D mixer couples across frequency scales.

Speed

CPU with 2 threads, batch 1, 128 mel bins, fp32; times in milliseconds. Softmax attention is parameter-free self-attention over the same tokens (every frame × 8-bin group).

  • The mixer's cost grows linearly with duration, attention's quadratically: at 4,096 frames (40.96 s of audio) the mixer is 5.4× faster.
  • The frame's cost is nearly flat in support: 35.9 ms with db1, 45.9 ms with db4 and 52.6 ms with db16 along time, at 1,024 frames.
  • Embedding all 2,000 ESC-50 clips (10,000 s of audio) takes 2,697 s on the same 2-thread CPU.

Run python benchmarks/speed.py --device cuda for GPU timings.
